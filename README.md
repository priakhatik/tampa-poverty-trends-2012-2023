# Tampa's Poverty Trends: A Decade of Change (2012–2023)

This project explores poverty trends across Florida counties over a 12-year period, with a focus on Hillsborough County (Tampa). Using data visualization and analysis, it highlights the economic challenges, recovery patterns, and county-level disparities shaped by broader socio-economic factors, including the COVID-19 pandemic.

## 📌 Project Objective

- Analyze poverty rate changes in Florida from **2012 to 2023**
- Highlight **Hillsborough County's progress** and comparison to neighboring counties
- Visualize the **impact of COVID-19** and post-pandemic recovery
- Identify counties with **notable improvements or worsening trends**

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Hillsborough County** recorded the **largest reduction** in poverty rate (~5.9%) among the selected counties.
- A **temporary spike in 2020** reflected the economic shock of the pandemic, followed by strong local recovery.
- In **2023**, Hillsborough’s poverty rate was **13.3%**, placing it in the middle tier—better than Citrus (17.2%) but higher than Sarasota (9.6%).

## 📄 Summary Report

Analyzing Florida’s Poverty Trends: Where Does Tampa (Hillsborough County) Stand?

Between 2012 and 2023, poverty rates across Florida counties shifted in response to economic
changes, the pandemic, and local recovery efforts. This report takes a closer look at
Hillsborough County – home to Tampa - to understand how its progress compares to that of
surrounding regions.

Key Insights:
Long-Term Trends: A Steady Decline with a Resilient Comeback
Over the past decade, Hillsborough saw a consistent drop in poverty rates. While 2020 brought
a temporary spike during the COVID-19 crisis, the county rebounded quickly showing signs of
strong community support and effective local programs.
2023 Snapshot: Tampa’s Position Among Peers
In the most recent data, Citrus County recorded the highest poverty rate, while Sarasota had
the lowest. Hillsborough landed in the middle faring better than many but still with room to
improve.
Decade of Change: Hillsborough Leads in Improvement
From 2012 to 2023, Hillsborough achieved the largest reduction in poverty nearly 6%. This
progress may be tied to economic diversification, increased access to services, and community-
driven efforts that helped residents recover and stay afloat.

Conclusion:
Tampa’s story stands out as one of growth and resilience. Despite statewide challenges,
Hillsborough County has made meaningful strides in reducing poverty. Continued focus on
equity, local investment, and inclusive support systems will be essential to building on this
momentum in the years ahead.
